How to cancel

OpenAI Help (help.openai.com/en/articles/7232927): web subscribers cancel via account menu > Settings > Billing > Cancel plan > Cancel; App Store / Google Play subscribers must cancel in that store. Uninstalling the app does NOT cancel. Cancel 24h+ before billing date.
