Free tier

Free tier exists (limits not verified)

Yes, a free version exists: Microsoft's page describes paid plans as having "higher usage than free" and offers "Try for free". Microsoft 365 Personal (with Copilot) is $9.99/mo; Family $12.99/mo. Free-tier limits not verified.

How to cancel

Official page: "Subscription automatically renews unless canceled in Microsoft account." A 1-month free trial is offered. Copilot is bundled with Office apps and 1 TB storage.
