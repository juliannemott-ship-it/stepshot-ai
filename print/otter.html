Usable free tier

Yes. Basic is free with 300 monthly transcription minutes and 3 lifetime file imports. Pro $16.99/user/mo monthly.

How to cancel

Otter Help: sign in as admin > Account Settings (Integrations) > Billing > Cancel Plan. Auto-renews; cancel 24h+ before billing date; non-refundable. App Store / Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled in the store.
