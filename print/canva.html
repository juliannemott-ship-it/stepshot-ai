Small AI allowance on free

Yes. Canva Free is US$0 with "Up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses". Pro US$144/year; Business US$250/year per person. Pro offers a free trial.

How to cancel

Canva Help: profile menu > Settings > Billing > Cancel plan > Continue cancellation > Cancel subscription. Plans auto-renew. App Store / Google Play / PayPal subscribers cancel there; uninstalling doesn't cancel. You can pause for 3 months instead.
